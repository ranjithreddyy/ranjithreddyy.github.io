## Concept

Futuristic cyborg × Japanese discipline.

- Cyborg layer: circuit motifs, neon-on-dark palette, monospace accents, animated counters, glowing profile artwork.
- Japanese layer: Kaizen, Shūren, Shokunin, Teinei as the About principles; restraint, spacing, and craft under the neon.
